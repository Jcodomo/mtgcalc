Handwriting and signatures

Type anything - a name, initials or a short note - and it is written by hand with our own pen engine, or make a signature by typing, drawing or uploading a PNG, SVG or photo. Everything you save here is offered on the signature lines of every document in the Document Builder.

How the pen works

  • The words are set in one of 20 handwriting styles, thinned to the centre line of each letter and traced into single pen strokes in writing order.
  • The strokes are then inked again: pressure that swells and tapers, a hand that wanders (sloppiness and legibility), a pen that runs dry (faded), uneven ink with paper grain, slant, opacity and stroke width.
  • Speed plays the writing back as it is written. New take writes the same words again, a little differently. Download as PNG, or as SVG made of real pen strokes.
  • Everything runs in this browser. Saved signatures are kept here only. A signature picture is not a verified e-signature - place one only on a document its signer has approved.
